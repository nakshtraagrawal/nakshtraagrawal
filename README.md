### 🧑‍💻 About me

I build computer vision systems that run **onboard drones**, from training the model to deploying it on a Jetson and flight-testing it in the field.

- 🛸 **Autonomous Aerial Systems Engineer** at **Comono India**: unsupervised aerial anomaly detection (ConvAutoencoder) running on a Jetson Orin Nano, with a continuous-learning feedback loop
- 🎯 **Imagery & Cyber Systems Lead** at **[Edhitha UAS](https://www.edhitha.com/)**: leading an 8-member subteam on detection, localisation and classification for international UAV competitions
- 🎓 B.E. Information Science & Engineering at **Ramaiah Institute of Technology**, Bengaluru (CGPA 9.31)
- 📍 Bengaluru, India
